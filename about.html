Particle physics, AI, and scientific discovery

I am a theoretical particle physicist and AI/ML researcher working at the intersection of particle-physics phenomenology, effective field theory, artificial intelligence, and scientific computing.

My physics research focuses on effective-field-theory interpretations of precision measurements, particularly SMEFT and WET, flavor physics, global analyses, and the systematic search for possible signatures of physics beyond the Standard Model.

In parallel, I develop and investigate AI-based methods for scientific research, including reinforcement learning, retrieval-augmented generation, large language models, and agentic AI systems. A central goal of this work is to build intelligent scientific tools that can connect domain knowledge, numerical software, experimental information, and automated reasoning.

I am also a contributor to open-source scientific software for effective field theory and particle-physics phenomenology, including wilson, WCxf, smelli, and flavio.
