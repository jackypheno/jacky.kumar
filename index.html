About

Physics, computation, and intelligent scientific tools

My work combines theoretical particle physics with modern AI/ML and scientific computing. I develop and use effective-field-theory tools to interpret precision measurements, and I explore reinforcement learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic AI as new methods for scientific discovery.
